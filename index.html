Questions? We've Got Answers.

Is there really no credit card required?

Yes! You can sign up and use all Pro features for 14 days completely free. We won't ask for payment details until you decide to upgrade.

How does the AI know what to do?

Our AI context engine analyzes your team's public workspace data (tasks, docs, public channels) to understand context, tone, and project status, ensuring high-quality, relevant outputs.

Can I import data from Notion or Monday?

Absolutely. We have 1-click importers for Notion, Monday.com, Asana, Jira, and Trello. Your structure and metadata will be preserved.

Is my data secure and private?

Security is our top priority. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and our AI models do not train on your private data.

What's the difference between Pro and Enterprise?

Enterprise adds advanced admin controls, SSO/SAML, dedicated customer success, custom SLA guarantees, and priority routing for support requests.
